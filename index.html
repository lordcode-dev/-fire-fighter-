Firefighter Robot

Complete offline wiring and build guide for an Arduino Uno robot using an L298N motor driver.

⚠️ IMPORTANT BEFORE BUILDING
This guide is designed for your team's planned robot. Check the labels on the actual L298N board before connecting power. L298N boards vary slightly. Never connect the motor battery directly to the Arduino 5V pin.

1 Movement

Build the basic 4-wheel robot and make it move.

🧰 Components required

🧠 Arduino Uno Main controller.
⚙️ L298N Motor driver.
🛞 4 × identical motors Two motors on each side.
🔋 Battery Motor power.
🟩 Breadboard Common-ground/control distribution.
🔌 Jumper wires Male-Male Male-Female if required

🌍 Common ground

Everything that uses the Arduino's control signals must share a common ground.

Battery − → Breadboard GND rail
Arduino GND → Breadboard GND rail
L298N GND → Breadboard GND rail

🧪 Stage 2 test order

3 Water Extinguishing

Keep Stages 1 and 2. Add the water system.

💧 Additional components

Water tank Securely mounted.
Water pump Must match its supply voltage/current.
Servo motor Aims the nozzle.
Water tube Connects tank to nozzle.
Nozzle Directs water at the flame.
MOSFET or relay module Switches the pump.

💧 System concept

⚠️ Pump warning: Never connect a water pump directly to an Arduino GPIO pin. Use an appropriate MOSFET/driver or relay and a suitable external pump supply.

4 Smoke Detection

Keep everything from Stages 1–3 and add smoke detection.

💨 Recommended beginner sensor

MQ-2 smoke/gas sensor module The MQ-2 can respond to smoke and several gases. It should be treated as supporting information rather than the only reason to activate the water pump.

🔌 MQ-2 basic wiring

MQ-2 Arduino
VCC 5V
GND Common GND
AO A0
MQ-2 modules need warm-up and calibration. The threshold should be determined experimentally.
